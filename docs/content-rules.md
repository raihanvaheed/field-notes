# Content rules

How pages in this repo are written. These apply to every file that gets published.

## What goes in
- What the speaker presented: their main ideas, examples and conclusions, in plain language.
- Links to the speaker's public papers, tools and pages.
- Diagrams redrawn to explain an idea, credited to the speaker.

## What stays out
- Anything about attendees or private conversations.
- Anything a speaker described as unpublished, preliminary or under review, unless it is now public.
- Anything that could not be confirmed. If a detail came through unclearly, it is dropped, not guessed.
- Photos of slides, speakers or the audience.
- Personal reflections and to-do lists.

## Voice
- Plain language. Explain terms the first time they appear.
- Attribute claims to the speaker ("She argued...", "He showed..."). Do not present them as settled fact.
- Numbers only when they are certain, with their source.

## Diagram credits
Every diagram gets one of these lines underneath it:
- `Adapted from <Speaker>'s talk at SMASH 2026.` plus a link to their paper if there is one.
- `Illustration of a point made by <Speaker> at SMASH 2026.` for ideas they explained without a figure.

## Status
Each talk file has `status: draft` until it has been checked against these rules, then `status: published`. Only published talks appear on the live page.
