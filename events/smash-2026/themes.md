# Ideas that kept coming up

Five threads ran through the talks I took notes on. Each talk page lists its themes, and the live page lets you trace each one across both days.

## Measure it properly

You can't improve what you don't measure, and you can't trust a number without knowing how it was measured. Luccioni and Gaud asked for real energy figures, Bacon for joules instead of tokens, and Lin showed the same model scoring very differently depending on the tool that tested it.

Talks: [Gaud](talks/gaud.md), [Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md), [El Emam](talks/el-emam.md), [Vanschoren](talks/vanschoren.md), [Bacon](talks/bacon.md), [Lin](talks/lin.md)

## Energy and footprint

AI's footprint is physical: electricity, grids, water, chips and the communities near data centres. Much of the waste is in running models rather than training them, and efficiency alone hasn't stopped total use from rising.

Talks: [Gaud](talks/gaud.md), [Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md), [Bacon](talks/bacon.md), [Lin](talks/lin.md)

## Checks that work

Safeguards only count if someone can actually use them. Speakers asked who notices quiet errors, whether people still think when AI explains itself, whether a robot's safety limits survive pressure, and whether delegating a decision to AI makes it legitimate.

Talks: [Doshi-Velez](talks/doshi-velez.md), [Panel](talks/panel.md), [Dilhac](talks/dilhac.md), [Bacon](talks/bacon.md), [Parra-Orlandoni](talks/parra-orlandoni.md), [Michel](talks/michel.md)

## AI in real hospitals

In healthcare the model is rarely the hard part. Workflow, roles, trust, privacy, integration and change management decide whether an AI tool helps anyone.

Talks: [Doshi-Velez](talks/doshi-velez.md), [El Emam](talks/el-emam.md), [Panel](talks/panel.md), [Kadivar](talks/kadivar.md)

## Open and shared

Shared datasets, metadata standards, open models and common formats for results let work add up instead of being rebuilt in every lab.

Talks: [Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md), [El Emam](talks/el-emam.md), [Vanschoren](talks/vanschoren.md), [Lin](talks/lin.md)
