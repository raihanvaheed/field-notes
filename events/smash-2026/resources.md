- [CodeCarbon](https://github.com/mlco2/codecarbon): measure the energy of your own code. ([Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md))
- [Power Hungry Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16863): energy use across AI tasks, 2023. ([Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md))
- [Estimating the Carbon Footprint of BLOOM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.02001), 2022. ([Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md))
- [Energy and Policy Considerations for Deep Learning in NLP](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02243), 2019. ([Luccioni](talks/luccioni.md))
- [Beyond Chinchilla-Optimal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00448): scaling laws that include the cost of running a model, 2023. ([Bacon](talks/bacon.md))
- [Flood Hub](https://sites.research.google/floodforecasting) and [Google Research on Flood Hub, FireSat and contrails](https://research.google/blog/how-ai-is-helping-us-build-a-more-resilient-planet/). ([Gaud](talks/gaud.md))
